The Philosophy

Heritage, carried forward by craft.

Kategna began with a single conviction: that the ingredients of the highlands — bright, sun-ripened, grown by families we still call by name — deserve technique as considered as their origin. Every plate is built the same way our grandmothers built a meal: slowly, with attention, and without shortcuts. We've simply given it a modern table to sit at.
